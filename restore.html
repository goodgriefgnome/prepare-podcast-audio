Generative models are slow, so they run on an excerpt. On a desktop CPU, Sidon and PASE take 1–2 s per second of audio, FlowSE about 30 s at its default 32 steps.
Models
Models from files on your disk These models know how speech sounds phoneme by phoneme. They’re too big to download here: get the files from the model release, then choose them below (all at once is fine). They’re read from your disk and never uploaded.
FlowSE settings
FlowSE’s vocabulary is pinyin plus letters and punctuation, used character by character, so English text is passed as letters.
